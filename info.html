Elect Karthik for Sammamish City Council 2025

It is time to rededicate our council and city government to our resident's welfare. I am running for the Sammamish City Council elections to bring focus back to resident needs, drive fiscal discipline and deliver an open and transparent governance.

The current council have burdened the residents by levying a 6% utility tax on top of increased property and utility bills and have abdicted their responsibility to govern by their total inaction and inadequate response during the bomb cyclone event. They have proposed to increase housing units in the town center to 4000 new homes which will result in overcrowded schools and increased traffic congestion with no solution possible.

Sammamish cannot be Redmond or Bellevue. Our urban forest needs active management from the city and the residents to maintain healthy trees that don’t damage our property. The council needs to work with utilities and Sound Transit/King County Metro and various companies in improving infrastructure in our city. I vow to prioritize improving quality of living for our current and future residents. Sammamish residents deserve a council member who will fight for their quality of life and their vision for the city's character. Vote Karthik in to realize this vision!
